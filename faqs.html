FAQs

Do you offer custom cakes?
Yes! We create custom cakes for birthdays, weddings and special events.
Do you cater for weddings?
Absolutely! We provide dessert catering for weddings and festivals.
Can I order online?
Yes, our online ordering system is coming soon!
Do you offer gluten-free options?
Yes, we have a selection of gluten-free cakes, brownies and pastries available daily.
Can I place a same-day order?
Same-day orders are available for selected items, subject to availability.
Do you make vegan desserts?
Yes! We offer a variety of delicious vegan cakes, cookies and cupcakes.
What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday to Saturday from 8am to 6pm, and Sundays from 9am to 4pm.
How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
We recommend ordering custom cakes at least 5 to 7 days in advance.
Do you deliver cakes and pastries?
Yes, we offer local delivery for custom cakes, dessert boxes and large orders.
Can you accommodate food allergies?
We do our best to accommodate allergies, but please contact us before ordering to discuss your requirements.
